    How it works

    You set the rules. Tafel does the Sunday-night maths.

    No meal-kit subscription and no imported recipes you can't shop for here. It's built from the ingredients on every South African shelf.

    1. 01

      Say what matters

      How many people you're feeding, what you can spend, and what's off the table. Vegetarian, halal, gluten-free, nut-free and more. Recipes that break a rule never appear.

      2 peopleR1 200 a weekNo porkLean towards high-protein
    2. 02

      Get a week that fits

      Breakfast, lunch and dinner for seven days, with a mix of chicken, red meat, fish and meat-free nights. Don't fancy Thursday? Swap it, and see exactly what it does to the total before you commit.

    3. 03

      Shop the list once

      Everything is grouped by aisle and rounded to real pack sizes, so you buy one 2 kg bag of potatoes, not "740 g". Tick items off as you go, or copy the list straight into WhatsApp.

    Prices

    The same week, priced at each store. Prices refresh on the 1st of every month.

    Training

    Easy run on Monday. Long run on Saturday. Your plate follows.

    Add your week of sessions and Tafel sets a daily protein target, scales your portions on the days you burn more, and tops up the list with eggs, yoghurt or tuna when meals fall short. Everyone else at the table eats as normal.

    protein a day for a 75 kg runner building strength
    kcal across the week, rest days included

    Dietary needs

    Built around how you actually eat.

    Your data stays yours

    Sign in with Google and your plans follow you from phone to laptop. No one else can see them, and without an account nothing leaves your browser.

    Straight to the store

    Every item links to its search at Checkers, Pick n Pay, Woolworths, Shoprite or Spar. Shop there as you always do, signed in to your own account.

    Honest about prices

    Totals are estimates from regular shelf prices, refreshed monthly. Specials will usually bring your bill in lower, never higher. Pay something different? Enter your own price and every plan uses it.

    Free

    No trial, no card, no catch. Open it in your browser and add it to your home screen.
