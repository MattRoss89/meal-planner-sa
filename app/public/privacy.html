What we collect

AccountYour name, email address and a Google account identifier, when you sign in with Google.
Planner settingsHousehold size, weekly budget, preferred store, dietary requirements, meal choices, prices you enter, saved plans and ticked-off list items.
Training details (optional)If you turn on training: weight, height, age, sex, goal and weekly sessions. Used only to calculate your food targets.
Cycle dates (optional)If you turn on cycle-aware planning, the date your last period started and your usual cycle and period length. These stay on your own device, in the app or your browser's storage. They are never sent to us or to our providers, and they're deleted when you clear them in the app or remove the app.
SubscriptionYour trial start date, subscription status, next billing date and the Paystack customer and subscription references. We never see or store your card number.
TechnicalBasic logs kept by our hosting provider (such as IP address and browser type) to run and secure the service.

Providing this information is voluntary. Without an account you can't save plans or subscribe; without training or cycle details those features stay off. Cycle information is health information, which POPIA treats as special personal information; by keeping it only on your device, we never collect or process it.

Why we use it

  • To create your meal plans, grocery lists and targets, and keep them in sync between your devices.
  • To run your free trial and subscription, and tell you about billing.
  • To keep the service secure and fix problems.

We rely on your consent and on performing our agreement with you. We don't sell your information, use it for advertising or send marketing without your separate consent.

Who we share it with

  • Google (Firebase) hosts the app, handles sign-in and stores your data. Your planner data is stored in Google's Johannesburg region; Google may process some data, such as sign-in, outside South Africa under its own data protection terms.
  • Paystack processes subscription payments and receives your email address and payment details directly. Paystack may process data outside South Africa under its own privacy policy and card-industry security standards.

We only share information with others where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and planner data while your account is active. If you ask us to delete your account, we delete it within 30 days. Records of payments are kept for five years, as tax law requires.

Security

Data travels over encrypted connections, and access rules mean only you can read or change your planner data. If a breach affects your information, we will tell you and the Information Regulator as POPIA requires.

Your rights

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, correct it, delete it or object to how we use it, by emailing [to be added: contact email]. We'll respond within 30 days. If you're unhappy with our response, you can complain to the Information Regulator at inforegulator.org.za.

Cookies and storage

Tafel uses your browser's storage to keep you signed in and remember your plan. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies.

Children

Tafel is a paid service intended for people aged 18 and over.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we'll tell you in the app before the change takes effect. Our PAIA manual is at /paia.html.
